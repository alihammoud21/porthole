# Code of conduct

## The standard

Be decent to people. Assume good faith. Keep disagreements about the work.

Porthole attracts a wide range of contributors — people who reverse-engineer 1990s file
formats, people who care about touch interaction design, people who just want to play
Morrowind on the train. All of that is welcome, and none of it requires being
unpleasant to anyone.

## Specifically expected

- Give feedback on the change, not the person who wrote it.
- Assume the other person knows something you don't, because on a project spanning
  iOS internals, emulator dynarecs, and 1996 asset layouts they almost certainly do.
- Accept that maintainers will say no to things, especially around the project's legal
  posture, and that "no" is not an insult.
- Write down what you learned. The next person is stuck on the same thing.

## Specifically not tolerated

- Harassment, slurs, or personal attacks
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome advances
- Publishing others' private information
- Sustained disruption, bad-faith argument, or pressure campaigns on maintainers
- Advocating for piracy in project spaces — not because anyone here is naive about it,
  but because it endangers the project's ability to exist and makes the maintainers'
  position indefensible

## Scope

Applies in this repository, its issues and PRs, and anywhere someone is representing
the project.

## Enforcement

Report to the maintainers via a private security advisory on this repository (see
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)) or by email to the address listed there. Reports are
handled confidentially.

Maintainers may warn, remove content, or ban, in proportion to what happened and
whether it's repeated.
